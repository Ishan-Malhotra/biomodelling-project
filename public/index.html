Enter internal coordinates

One atom per line. Lengths in Å, angles in degrees. The bond is to the previous atom, the angle with the one before that, and the dihedral with the one before that.

To choose reference atoms yourself, put the atom number before each value: CB  2 1.530  1 110.5  3 -122.6. Lines starting with # are ignored.

or press Ctrl/⌘ + Enter

Build a backbone (N, CA, C atoms) from φ, ψ and ω. Bond lengths and angles use ideal values (Engh & Huber): N–CA 1.458 Å, CA–C 1.525 Å, C–N 1.329 Å; N–CA–C 111.2°, CA–C–N 116.2°, C–N–CA 121.7°.

The first residue has no φ and the last has no ψ or ω, because those angles need atoms from a neighbouring residue.
